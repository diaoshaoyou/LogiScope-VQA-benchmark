Introduction

LogiScope-VQA is the first dedicated benchmark for evaluating Large Multimodal Models (LMMs) on logistics hazard identification in real-world industrial scenarios. Built from 3.5 million surveillance clips collected over one year across the global warehouse parks of Cainiao, a leading intelligent logistics company, every question is grounded in real-world warehouse safety regulations.

Key Findings

1
Fine-grained perception remains a major bottleneck. Models struggle with low-resolution, wide-angle, heavily occluded and densely cluttered footage, falling below even novice humans.
2
Safety risk bias is pervasive yet overlooked. Most models show a conservative (over-reporting) bias; even the strongest fall far short of production-grade readiness and human experts.
